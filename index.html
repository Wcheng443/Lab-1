Our Mission

Habitat for Humanity believes that every man, woman and child should have a decent, safe and affordable place to live. We build and repair houses using volunteer labor and donations.

Why We Build

Nearly 2 billion people worldwide live in poor housing conditions. Habitat provides shelter and housing assistance to families recovering from disaster, conflict, and poverty.
